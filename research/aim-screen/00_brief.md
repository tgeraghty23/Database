# Brief: AIM screen for investable assets for Corsair Capital

Date: 4 November 2026. Owner: this session. Method: METHOD.md v0.4.

## Question
Which companies quoted on London's AIM market could be investable for Corsair Capital's buyouts platform, and
what does the AIM pool as a whole offer Corsair today?

## Intended reader and use
A Corsair deal professional deciding where to spend origination time. The output is a ranked long list with the
reasons for each ranking, not an investment recommendation. It should let the reader pick three to five names
for first-look work and understand why the rest were set aside.

## What the answer must explain
- Corsair's actual mandate as the filter (verticals, size, deal types), not a generic "financial services" label.
- Which AIM names clear that filter on business fit, size and ownership, and which fail and why.
- What recent AIM take-private activity says about price, competition and the odds of completing a deal.
- Where the fit is partial (add-on to an existing Corsair platform, minority or PIPE rather than take-private).

## Provisional scope
- Universe: all AIM-quoted companies as at early November 2026 (about 595), filtered to Corsair's three verticals.
- Excludes Main Market companies, Aquis-quoted companies and private targets, except as context.
- Treats AIM precedent deals from January 2024 as the comparable set.

## Material unknowns at the start
- Corsair's current fund size and remaining dry powder (public figures conflict: about USD 470m to 500m for Fund VI).
- Whether Corsair would do a UK take-private at all; its listed-company precedents are not obvious.
- Live data: no CapIQ or FactSet connector in this session, so market caps and financials come from public pages.

## Assumptions stated
- "Corsair Capital" means the New York and London financial-services buyout firm (corsair-capital.com), not the
  hedge fund Corsair Capital Management LP. The user's email domain supports this.
- "Investable" means a potential platform, add-on or significant minority at an enterprise value broadly within
  Corsair's stated USD 150m to 750m range, with some latitude below the floor for add-ons.
- Market caps are the stockanalysis.com AIM list read on 4 November 2026 unless an agent found a better source.

## What would count as answering the wrong question
- A list of the largest AIM financials regardless of Corsair's verticals.
- A list of "cheap" AIM stocks with no view on whether a control deal is achievable.
- Treating a company already under offer or moved to the Main Market as available.
