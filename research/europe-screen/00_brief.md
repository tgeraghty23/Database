# Brief: Continental and Nordic public-market screen for Corsair Capital

Date: 4 November 2026. Owner: this session. Method: METHOD.md v0.4. Companion to research/aim-screen/ (same filter).

## Question
Which listed companies in the Nordics (Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland), Spain, Germany, Italy and France could be
investable for Corsair Capital's buyouts platform, and how does each market's pool compare with AIM?

## Filter (unchanged from the AIM screen)
Corsair verticals: payments (integrated, international, B2B); software (vertical and enterprise, risk and compliance,
HR and finance tools); business services (wealth and asset management, insurance services, professional services,
healthcare services, trust and corporate services). Enterprise value USD 150m to 750m (about EUR 130m to 650m at 1.15),
larger with co-investors; platform, add-on or minority with control or real influence; modest leverage.

## Scope
All regulated and growth markets in the five areas: Nasdaq Stockholm/Helsinki/Copenhagen, First North, Oslo Børs and
Euronext Growth Oslo, Frankfurt (Prime/General Standard, Scale), Euronext Paris and Euronext Growth Paris, Borsa
Italiana (Euronext Milan, STAR, Euronext Growth Milan), BME (Mercado Continuo, BME Growth). Banks and balance-sheet
lenders are context only (outside Corsair's stated verticals). Insurers are included only where the value is in
distribution or services, not underwriting capital.

## Assumptions
- Corsair means the financial-services buyout firm (corsair-capital.com). EV band is from a third-party summary.
- Market caps are the latest available public price on or near 4 November 2026; FX at EUR/USD 1.15, SEK/EUR 11.0,
  NOK/EUR 11.6, DKK/EUR 7.46 unless an agent sources a better rate.
- Coverage is seed list plus exchange sector lists; it cannot be exhaustive without a data terminal. Agents state
  what they scanned.

## What would count as answering the wrong question
A list of the largest financials per country; a list of cheap stocks with no view on deliverability; names already
under offer or delisted.
