# Nordic and continental public-market screen for Corsair Capital

4 November 2026. Research note under METHOD.md v0.4, companion to the AIM screen of the same date. Data:
`data/europe_screen_shortlist_2026-11-04.csv`; evidence in `agent-returns/` (six market files, one precedent file).
Figures in EUR at the brief's rates (SEK 11.0, NOK 11.6, DKK 7.46 per EUR; USD 1.15); prices are delayed closes on
4 November 2026 from a data site, not a terminal.

## Summary

The five markets together hold about a dozen names that are on-vertical, inside Corsair's USD 150m to 750m enterprise-
value band and have a register that can be worked. That is twice the AIM count, but spread across six jurisdictions and
mostly anchored by a strategic or family holder of 20 to 50 percent, so almost every one is a negotiation with a named
counterparty rather than a market tender. The best fits by vertical are JDC Group (Germany, insurance and wealth
platform, anchored by Great-West Lifeco), Enento (Finland, credit and KYC data, where media group Otava's 26.5 percent
stake-build is the live control event), eQ and CapMan (Finnish asset managers), Equita and Alantra (Italian and Spanish
asset-management-plus-advisory partnerships), Infotel and Sidetrade (France, financial-services IT and finance software)
and Lime Technologies (Sweden, deliverable but expensive).

The surprise is price. Continental take-private premiums since 2024 have been higher than the UK's, not lower: Swedish
bids averaged about 50 percent in 2025, German software deals cleared at 33 to 53 percent, and the direct analogue for
Corsair's distribution thesis, Netfonds, went to Warburg Pincus in 2026 at 64 percent over the undisturbed price. The
mechanics differ too: Sweden's 90 percent redemption threshold lets holdout funds kill a recommended bid (Karnov 2024),
Germany is run as a 50 to 75 percent minimum offer plus a delisting offer, and France, Italy and Spain are controller-led.

My current view is that Corsair's time is best spent on four conversations rather than a bid list: Great-West Lifeco
about JDC; Otava and Enento's board; the Ehrnrooth and Rettig anchors at eQ (or CapMan's dispersed register); and the
founders of Infotel and Sidetrade. Karnov and GPI are live sale situations above the band that Corsair should track as a
co-investor, not lead. The weakest premise is the EV band, which is third-party sourced; if Corsair goes lower, Finland's
and Sweden's sub-band software and adviser names become platform material. Coverage is a stated limit: the universe was
built from seed lists and exchange sector pages, not a terminal, so a dispersed in-band name outside the seeds could be
missing, most likely in Germany's Scale segment or Sweden's First North.

## 3. Price and mechanics from 45 precedents

| Market | Premiums observed 2024-26 | Mechanics that decide deliverability |
|---|---|---|
| Sweden | c.52% average 2025, c.43% H1 2026; Fortnox 38%, Byggfakta 48% after bump, Formpipe 54% | 90% compulsory redemption; holdouts decisive (Karnov withdrawn; Resurs and Sleep Cycle closed below 90% with conditions waived) |
| Norway | Zalaris at NOK 100 closed at 86.7% | 90% rule bypassed by a two-thirds EGM delisting vote |
| Germany | Datagroup 33%, Nexus c.44%, CompuGroup c.51%, EQS c.53%, Netfonds 64% | 95% squeeze-out rarely reached; 50-75% minimum offer then BorsG delisting offer; sponsors live with 67-87% stakes |
| France | Esker c.30%; founder squeeze-outs 35-200% | 90% retrait obligatoire reached via pre-committed blocks; a >10% minority concert can block (Keyrus) |
| Italy | 2-22% in bank-led deals; Almawave 37% | 90% sell-out and 95% squeeze-out; top-ups to cross 90%; loyalty shares |
| Spain | Catalana Occidente c.18-19%; Applus c.35% in a contested auction | 30% mandatory bid; 90/90 squeeze-out; delisting via AGM exclusion offer |

Sponsors active in these verticals: EQT, CVC, KKR, TA, Carlyle, Thoma Bravo, Warburg Pincus, Bridgepoint and General
Atlantic, Cinven, Stirling Square, Altor, Triton, Norvestor, CapMan, Bregal, One Equity Partners, Advent and Nextalia.
Strategics (Thomson Reuters, Visma, Ambea, VINCI, Banca Generali, Banco BPM, Banca Ifis, Nykredit, S-Bank) have won
several auctions on price. For a Corsair-sized target the in-band comparables (Netfonds, Zalaris, Datagroup, SNP, EQS,
All for One, Humana, Formpipe, Pagero, Intermonte) say: expect a 30 to 50 percent premium, a pre-committed block of
20 to 50 percent, and a two-step route in Germany and Norway versus a single 90 percent hurdle in Sweden, France and Italy.

AIM is cheaper and simpler to execute; the continent has more on-vertical assets but each comes with a counterparty
and a higher price. The two screens are complementary: Boku, Fintel, FRP and BTG for UK execution; JDC, Enento, eQ,
Infotel and Sidetrade for relationship-led European work.

## 5. Implications

- Open four conversations: Great-West Lifeco (JDC), Otava and Enento, eQ's family anchors (with CapMan as the dispersed
  alternative), and the founders of Infotel and Sidetrade.
- Track as co-investor, not lead: Karnov (Goldman process) and GPI (KKR contacts).
- Partnership models, not take-privates: Equita (Iccrea template), Alantra (partner pacts), Evli, United Bankers, Atrys.
- Add-on list for IDnow (Facephi, Freja eID, Wallix) and for a finance-tools platform (Exsitec, Lemonsoft, Seresco).
- Do not expect listed payments assets in these markets; Corsair's payments thesis stays private-market or UK.

## 6. Uncertainty and what would change the view

- EV band is third-party sourced (see AIM note). A lower floor adds Finnish and Swedish sub-band names as platforms.
- Coverage: seeds plus exchange lists, not a terminal; Germany's Scale segment and Sweden's First North were covered
  by seeds only. A missed dispersed in-band name is the most likely error.
- Karnov's sale review rests on call summaries, not a company release. GPI's PE talks are 2025 press with no 2026
  update. Both are obtainable from the companies.
- Profit measures are mixed (EBITDA, EBIT, operating profit, often data-site TTM including IFRS 16); multiples in this
  note are indicative and must be rebuilt from annual reports before any bid arithmetic.
- Ownership figures are dated 2024-26 and in some cases from aggregators; free float is null for most Nordic names.
- The provisional premise that continental premiums run below the UK was contradicted by the precedent set and the
  argument was revised accordingly; the ranking does not depend on it, the price expectation does.

Established: which names are listed, their size and anchors; the precedent premiums and mechanics. Best current
judgment: the ranking and the four conversations. Conditional: multiples and price arithmetic. Open: whether Corsair
will lead a continental public-to-private at all, given no precedent on record.
