# European private higher education: market map and shortlist

Prepared 22 October 2026 for a control-buyout mandate at EUR 50m to 300m enterprise value. Companion files: `operators.xlsx` (203 operators, 196 brands, weighted scores), `precedents.xlsx` (168 transaction and financing rows, 2015 to 2026), `country_matrix.xlsx` (30 countries), `memo.md`, `sources.md`.

Confidence note: roughly one operator in four has a disclosed revenue figure. Most German, Polish, Cypriot and Italian operators carry "n/a" with the registry named that holds the accounts. Every score in `operators.xlsx` is flagged disclosed, estimate or mixed. The web-search budget ran out part-way through every workstream, so the long tail was verified by direct page fetches rather than fresh searches.

## 4. How the map was built and what is missing

- 203 operators across 30 countries, scored on six dimensions with weights in the `Scoring method` sheet.
- Filed revenue or EBITDA was retrieved for roughly 50 operators. The rest need registry pulls: Companies House OCR (UK scanned PDFs), Bundesanzeiger (Germany), Registro Mercantil (Spain), KRS (Poland), Cyprus registrar, Infogreffe (France), Cerved (Italy).
- Several anchor assumptions in the original brief were wrong and are corrected in the data: Universidad Europea is EQT-owned, not in a Permira sale; UAX went to Cinven and Mubadala in Oct 2025; UNIR delisted in 2025; Ilerna is Jacobs-owned, not Ardian; MEDAC and MasterD are KKR's Davante; AKAD is Galileo-owned; Modul Vienna is owned by Hungary's MCC; ISM Lithuania is controlled by KTU; AD Education's prior owners were founder plus Cathay and Bpifrance, not Eurazeo or Sagard.
