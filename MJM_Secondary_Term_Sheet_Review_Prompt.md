# Prompt: consistency and entity review of the MJM Secondary Term Sheet

Attached are (1) the current draft "MJM – Secondary Term Sheet" (v20261027_2310) and (2) the MJM group structure chart. The term sheet is a deliberately simplified, illustrative, non‑binding term sheet for a secondary purchase by Corsair European Insurance Services L.P. ("CEIS") of Effinity's shares in Denali Pooling I SCA. Please review it and report back. Do not redraft the document or add new commercial terms; this is a consistency and accuracy review only.

## 1. Entity check against the structure chart

For every entity named in the term sheet, confirm against the structure chart that the legal name, legal form, jurisdiction and position in the chain are correct, and list any discrepancy with the exact text to change. Specifically confirm:

- **Buyer:** "Corsair European Insurance Services L.P." Is this the correct Corsair vehicle that sits in the Denali chain on the chart? Is CEIS actually a shareholder in Denali Pooling (so that it acquires alongside Effinity at the same level), or does it hold at Denali I or elsewhere? Confirm the defined‑term approach "(together with its affiliates, "CEIS")" is consistent with the vehicle on the chart.
- **Seller:** "Efinity Insurance Group Spółka Ackyjna". Check the spelling of the entity name (one f or two) and of the legal form ("Spółka Akcyjna" / "S.A."), and make the defined term "Effinity" and the signature block consistent with the correct legal name.
- **Target company of the Sale Shares:** "Denali Pooling I SCA" ("Denali Pooling"). Confirm the full legal name and form (SCA, SCSp, etc.), jurisdiction, and that Effinity's shares sit at this entity rather than at Denali I.
- **Denali I:** "Denali Holding I S.a.r.L". Confirm the correct form and capitalisation (S.à r.l.) and its place in the chain between Denali Pooling and MJM.
- **MJM Holdings:** confirm this is the correct name of the operating holding company and that Denali I is its indirect (not direct) holding company as stated.
- Any other entities on the chart that should be referenced but are not (for example an intermediate holding company between Denali I and MJM, or a Denali EIS‑type vehicle through which CEIS holds), and whether their absence creates any inaccuracy in the term sheet.

Produce a short table: entity as drafted | entity per structure chart | action.

## 2. Denali Pooling vs Denali I consistency

The term sheet moves between Denali Pooling and Denali I. Check every reference and flag where the wrong entity is used. Points to test:

- The opening paragraph says the Sale Shares are shares in Denali Pooling, but the Transaction Overview says CEIS acquires the Sale Shares "in Denali I". These should agree.
- The Sale Shares section correctly refers to Denali Pooling for the share classes and the 23.9% / 25.2% figures, and to Denali I for the 6.6% / 6.3% look‑through figures. Confirm the share classes (Strip Ordinary and A–H Preference Shares) actually exist at Denali Pooling level per the chart, or whether they mirror Denali I classes.
- Transaction Overview: "No new shares will be issued by Denali I or the Company" should also cover Denali Pooling.
- Conditions to Completion refers to consents under "the SHA and the Denali I constitutional documents", whereas the transfer takes place at Denali Pooling. Confirm which constitutional documents and which shareholder agreement govern transfers of Denali Pooling shares, and whether the Denali Holding I SHA dated 28 March 2024 actually applies at Denali Pooling level or a separate Denali Pooling agreement is relevant.
- Rights Attaching to the Sale Shares, Warranties and Documentation refer to Denali Pooling. Confirm consistent.
- Valuation: the Purchase Price is derived from the SHA waterfall at Denali I. Confirm that applying the Denali I waterfall and then looking through to Denali Pooling is the right mechanic, and flag if a Denali Pooling‑level waterfall or pooling arrangement should be referenced instead.
