## 4. Conclusion

The term sheet is close to internally consistent and, apart from two entity-name points, reflects the structure chart. The must-fix items are confined to five places: the three Denali I references that should read Denali Pooling (C1, C2, C3), the Reference Date and source-document definition (C4), and the Effinity legal name and spelling (C5). Once those are made, the only open points of substance are the choice of buyer (CEIS directly versus Denali EIS, which the chart cannot settle), the Denali Holding versus Denali Holdings name, the 6.3% ordinary look-through figure, and the accrued-coupon mechanic in the Purchase Price. None of these requires new commercial terms; each is a confirmation against the registers, the SHA or the model. With the must-fix changes made, the document reads as a coherent two-party secondary term sheet and nothing from the primary-investment version survives.
