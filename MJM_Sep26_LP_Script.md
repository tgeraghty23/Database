# MJM Holdings – September 2026 LP Talking Script

*Purpose: spoken walk-through of the September 2026 trading update for an LP call. All figures PLN, Adj. EBITDA unless stated. Source: September 2026 Board Reporting Pack and the trading summary email.*

---

## 1. Opening frame (30 seconds)

"Let me take you through September. The short version: the group is growing strongly year on year, the LTM numbers are the best we've had, and leverage is stable. But September itself was a soft month against budget, driven by two lumpy broker placements at Smartt and Attis that either slipped or were lost. I'll be upfront about those, then walk you through where the recovery comes from and what's happening on M&A."

---

## 2. Headline numbers

**LTM to September 2026**
- Revenue PLN 294m, up 31% year on year.
- Adj. EBITDA PLN 79.8m, up 35% year on year, at a 27% margin.

"So on a trailing basis, margin is expanding, not just revenue. EBITDA is growing faster than the top line."

**September month**
- Revenue PLN 21.8m, up 42% year on year.
- Adj. EBITDA PLN 3.0m, up 17% year on year, 14% margin.

"The month was up on last year but well short of budget. Budget for September was PLN 5.3m of EBITDA, so we delivered about 55% of plan in the month."

**Year to date (nine months)**
- Adj. EBITDA PLN 45.6m vs PLN 57.1m budget, so 20% behind plan, but 22% ahead of last year.

"I want to be clear on how to read that gap. It is not a cost problem. Year to date, staff costs and indirect costs are both running below budget, in total around PLN 13m of savings. The entire miss is revenue, and the revenue miss is concentrated in the two specialist brokers, Smartt and Attis. Everything else is at or ahead of plan."

---

## 3. Divisional walk

### GIA (the affinity and association business) – the engine

- September EBITDA PLN 4.2m at a 45% margin, 23% ahead of budget and 45% up year on year.
- LTM EBITDA PLN 45.3m, up 38%, at a 48% margin.

"GIA is now well over half of group EBITDA and it's beating plan. Three things are driving it:

First, the core GSU Association book is stable. GWP of PLN 23.3m in September, half a million above budget, and nearly PLN 5m ahead of budget year to date.

Second, pricing and cross-sell. Average monthly premium per member has gone from roughly PLN 88 at the start of 2025 to PLN 103 now. Home assistance penetration has gone from 39% to 67%, and the personal liability product we launched in April is already at 11% penetration. That's where the margin comes from.

Third, the non-association broker channel delivered PLN 1.1m above budget on renewals and two new guarantee placements."

**What to flag honestly:** "Member count is still drifting down, about 226k against a 238k budget. New acquisition picked up to 380 in September, the best month this year, helped by the PZU partnership starting to produce, but churn still outpaces it. We're managing this as a yield-and-cross-sell story rather than a volume story, and the numbers show that works. But member count is the KPI to watch."

### Smartt (specialist corporate broker) – the September miss

- September EBITDA negative PLN 1.0m against PLN 2.0m budget.
- LTM EBITDA PLN 16.5m, up 62% year on year, 33% margin.

"Smartt is the biggest single reason September missed. Two items explain essentially all of it:

One, we lost the Gaz System account. That was about PLN 2.1m of gross profit budgeted in September.

Two, the Tauron wind farm placement did not proceed. That's another PLN 1m.

Here's the offset. Tauron Wytwarzanie, the generation business, renewed in October at around PLN 9m of revenue against PLN 5m in budget. The co-broker split on that is still being settled, so I can't give you a precise net number yet, but it's a meaningful October positive. Beyond that, PKP PLK and PSE have both renewed well ahead of plan this year, we've submitted a property tender for Orlen, PEJ's nuclear placement is expected around year end at circa PLN 8m, and Baltic Pipe is an unbudgeted win already worth over half a million.

So the LTM number, up 62%, is the right lens. The monthly numbers at Smartt will always be lumpy because of placement timing."

### Attis (specialist broker, marine and surety) – timing plus a restructuring

- September EBITDA negative PLN 0.1m against PLN 1.1m budget.
- LTM EBITDA PLN 17.7m, up 10% year on year, 32% margin.

"Attis is a project-timing story. The year-to-date gross profit gap to budget is about PLN 13m, and the largest pieces are two CRIST jack-up vessel placements, together over PLN 6m, that have been pushed into 2027. Those are postponed, not lost.

Given the revenue shortfall, management has run a restructuring targeting PLN 1.4m of annualised savings. That covers three positions in Warsaw, the sales team, and closing the Rzeszów branch. None of that is in the September numbers yet. Headcount at Attis is already 70 against a budget of 80."

### MJM Brokers (fleet and motor) – small, but beating plan

- September EBITDA PLN 1.0m at a 27% margin, against a PLN 0.24m budget.
- LTM EBITDA PLN 3.2m, down 14% year on year, 9% margin.

"Small contributor but a good month. September was four times budget, driven by P&C cross-sell into Attis. Year to date it is still behind plan because the Superauto sales-broker partnership started late and we haven't added new rent-a-car fleet. The LTM decline is the restructuring from earlier in the year washing through, and the monthly run-rate is now improving."

### Leadenhall (life and MGA) – in line with budget, still investing

- September EBITDA negative PLN 0.6m, in line with budget, on PLN 4.4m revenue.
- LTM EBITDA PLN 4.0m, 7% margin, in line with budget, on PLN 53m revenue.

"Leadenhall is tracking plan. The heritage life book is doing well: GWP year to date of PLN 81m is 97% of plan and 23% up on last year, renewal rates are holding around 90%, and the heritage business was actually PLN 0.26m better than budget in September.

The drag is the MGA, which is a start-up. GWP is at 57% of plan year to date. Motor is strong, a record PLN 6.8m in September, but the corporate book is behind because QBE capacity approvals have been slow. That's being worked, with the QBE binder renewed and alternative capacity in discussion. We're holding costs tight there, roughly 30% below budget, so the EBITDA impact is contained."

### Holdings costs – below budget

- LTM EBITDA negative PLN 8.0m against negative PLN 10.1m budget.

"Central costs are running about PLN 2m below budget on an LTM basis. September was PLN 0.5m against PLN 0.9m budgeted."

## 4. Balance sheet and leverage

- Net debt to Adj. EBITDA 4.20x at September, versus 4.21x in August.
- Covenant level 5.75x.
- Net debt PLN 335m, cash PLN 16m.

"Leverage is flat month on month at 4.2x against a 5.75x covenant, so roughly 1.5 turns of headroom. The budget has us ending the year at 4.24x, so no deterioration expected.

A couple of cash items worth knowing. September operating cash flow was PLN 3.1m, but cash fell by PLN 3.4m overall. We funded the Certo acquisition in August with PLN 10m of our own cash, and we've since drawn EUR 2.5m under the acquisition facility to refinance that. That landed in October. We also put PLN 4.2m into Leadenhall Fleet in early October under the signed SPA.

Headcount is 519 against a budget of 531, so we're not over-hiring. Eighteen joiners and fourteen leavers in September."

---

## 5. M&A and platform

**Maximus Broker**
"Signing is expected early next week, closing at the end of December. Maximus is the leading broker to local governments and municipalities in Poland. Last year it wrote PLN 180m of GWP, PLN 26m of revenue and PLN 8.6m of EBITDA."

**Unity**
"Non-binding terms are agreed. Due diligence starts at the end of November and signing is possible in the first quarter. Unity is a trade credit specialist, around PLN 11m of revenue and PLN 5m of EBITDA."

**Pipeline**
"Behind those, we're in early discussions with Invictus, a guarantees broker, and Inter Broker, which would be highly synergistic with Maximus and would make us the clear number one in the public-sector segment."

**Broker system**
"The group-wide broker platform is the one project I'd flag as slipping. Go-live is moving from late 2026 to January 2027. Functional readiness is at 73% and data migration at 30%, both up on last month. The scope and cost are fixed; it's a testing capacity issue on our side, not a vendor or budget issue."

---

## 6. Closing

"To sum up:

The group is 31% bigger and 35% more profitable than a year ago on an LTM basis, with margin at 27%.

September was a soft month against plan, almost entirely two placements at Smartt and one timing issue at Attis. One of those has already been more than offset by the Tauron renewal in October.

GIA, which is more than half the group, is beating plan on yield and cross-sell.

Costs are below budget across the board and there's a further PLN 1.4m of Attis savings to come.

Leverage is stable at 4.2x with 1.5 turns of covenant headroom.

And Maximus signs next week, which brings a scaled, profitable public-sector broker into the group."

## 7. Likely LP questions and suggested answers

**"You're 20% behind budget year to date. Will you hit the full-year number?"**
"We will not hit the original budget. Budget was PLN 79m for the full year and we're at PLN 45.6m after nine months. Q4 is seasonally the strongest quarter, Tauron is a PLN 9m revenue item landing in October, and PEJ could land in December. But the honest answer is the full-year outcome will be below plan and well above last year. LTM is already PLN 79.8m, so the business is at the budget run-rate on a trailing basis; the gap is timing of the specialist broker placements."

**"How much of the 35% LTM EBITDA growth is organic?"**
"A meaningful part is acquisition-driven. GIA came in July 2025 and Certo in August 2026, so the LTM comparison includes partial-year contributions. Like-for-like, the specialist brokers are growing: Smartt up 62% and Attis up 10% LTM. GIA was in the comparison for part of last year, and its own LTM growth is 38%. I'd rather give you a clean organic split than estimate it on the call, so let me follow up with that." *(Note: the pack does not provide an organic/inorganic split. Do not quote a number.)*

**"Was Gaz System lost on price or service?"**
*(The pack says "Lost" without a reason. Do not speculate. Suggested line:)* "I'll come back to you with the specifics from the Smartt team. What I can say is that the relationships that matter most, PKP PLK, PSE, Tauron, PGNiG, all renewed ahead of plan this year."

**"Is the GSU membership decline a structural problem?"**
"It's the one KPI at GIA that's going the wrong way, and we're watching it. Net loss is about 13k members year to date. The offsets are real: average premium up 17% since January 2025 and cross-sell penetration more than doubled. New acquisition hit a 2026 high in September at 380, and the PZU partnership is only just starting to contribute. If acquisition stabilises at that level, the book stabilises. If not, we have a yield story that eventually runs out of road, and that's why the PZU channel matters."

**"Why is Leadenhall MGA so far behind plan?"**
"Capacity. The QBE binder has been in force since September but key underwriting approvals on property and liability are outstanding. Motor is working, a record month in September. We're pursuing alternative capacity from Korean Re, CapacityPlace and AON. We've kept the cost base about 30% below budget, so the EBITDA cost of the delay is small."

**"What's the covenant headroom in EBITDA terms?"**
"At PLN 335m of net debt, covenant EBITDA would need to fall to about PLN 58m to breach 5.75x. LTM is PLN 79.8m. That's a 27% EBITDA cushion."

**"How are you funding Maximus and Unity?"**
*(The pack does not state the purchase price or funding structure for either deal. Do not quote. Suggested line:)* "Through the acquisition facility, consistent with how we funded Certo. I'll share the funding structure once Maximus is signed."

**"Why is the broker system slipping?"**
"Our own testing capacity, not the vendor. Scope and cost are fixed. The vendor's backlog review puts readiness at 73%, up from 71% last month. The go-live move to January is within the fixed MVP contract. It's a two-month slip, not a re-plan."
